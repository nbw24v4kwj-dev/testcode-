Overview

DocCode respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your data. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and how we keep it secure.

Data we collect

  • Account information (name, company, email)
  • Billing details and transaction records
  • Usage analytics for product improvement
  • Support tickets and service communications

How we use data

We use data to deliver services, process payments, improve templates, and provide compliance-ready audit trails for customers.

Security

We apply encryption in transit and at rest, role-based access controls, and continuous monitoring to protect data integrity.

Your rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your data. Enterprise customers can configure data retention policies in the account center.
