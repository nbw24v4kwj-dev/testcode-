Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2025-01-15

Overview

DocCode provides documentation templates, code packs, and workflow tooling for enterprise delivery teams. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and safeguard personal data across our sites, services, and customer workspaces.

Information we collect

  • Account and profile data (name, role, company, work email, region).
  • Billing and transaction details processed by approved payment providers.
  • Workspace activity logs, template usage, and audit events.
  • Support communications and security verification data.

How we use information

  • Deliver services, authenticate users, and manage subscriptions.
  • Maintain audit trails and compliance-ready reporting.
  • Improve templates, code modules, and documentation quality.
  • Provide customer support and security notifications.

Data sharing and transfers

We share data with infrastructure, analytics, and payment partners who support service delivery. Transfers are governed by contractual safeguards and data processing agreements.

Security measures

  • Encryption in transit and at rest for sensitive data.
  • Role-based access controls and least-privilege policies.
  • Continuous monitoring, anomaly detection, and audit logging.

Your rights

You may request access, correction, export, or deletion of your personal data, subject to legal and contractual requirements. Enterprise administrators can configure retention and access policies in the account center.

Contact

Questions or requests can be sent to privacy@doccode.io.

Agreement

By accessing DocCode, you agree to these Terms of Service. These terms apply to individual users, enterprise customers, and authorized team members using DocCode workspaces.

Subscriptions and access

  • Subscriptions grant access to templates, code packs, and updates during the active term.
  • Seat counts and workspace permissions are managed by account owners.
  • Trials or pilot access may include feature limits described at checkout.

Billing and renewals

Fees are billed in advance based on the selected plan. Renewal settings are available in the account center, and invoices are provided for eligible plans.

Acceptable use

  • No unauthorized redistribution of templates or code outside licensed teams.
  • No attempts to bypass access controls, rate limits, or security measures.
  • No use of the service for unlawful, harmful, or misleading activity.

Intellectual property

DocCode retains all rights to its templates, software, and content. Customers receive a non-exclusive license to use materials in accordance with their plan.

Termination

We may suspend or terminate access for material violations of these terms. Customers may cancel renewal via the account center, with access lasting through the current billing period.
